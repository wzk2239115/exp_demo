In the pooling instance allocator (crates/runtime/src/instance/allocator/pooling.rs), when InstanceLimits::memory_pages is configured to zero the allocator does not establish the virtual-memory mapping and guard pages that the compiler relies on to bounds-check linear-memory accesses, so a guest module can perform out-of-bounds reads and writes against memory adjacent to the (effectively empty) linear-memory region and escape the sandbox.
