## Anti-patterns to avoid

- **Deep-diving a source path past the point where the hypothesis is dead (e.g., ~20 steps on EmptyFixedArray after concluding it yields no primitive)**: when progress stalls on variant 2-3 of a hypothesis, force a switch to another candidate.
