## Anti-patterns to avoid
- **Repeated attempts to debug with GDB/core dumps after ptrace and ulimit fail**: switch to static analysis or alternate dynamic tools.
- **Long cycles reading `MergeValue`/`CopyFrom`/`MergeValue` source without new evidence**: if you cannot explain a behavior after 3 source passes, change your observation tool (e.g., trace with smaller inputs).
- **Repeatedly running `--trace-maglev-graph-building` when it crashes at a known point**: try smaller case counts or different flags first.
- **Dumping huge graphs (e.g., 65535-case functions) and timing out**: use targeted greps or smaller reproductions to avoid 90s timeouts.
- **Analyzing switch dispatch in the binary's assembly when the problem is at graph build**: this yields no new hypotheses; stay in the source/trace layer.
